**Entry criteria:** Process owner authorizes the bounded pilot; access approvals are verified; representative observation occurs; the success definition and quality checks are agreed; support cover, fallback, and reconciliation responsibilities are assigned. The team's ability to operate the service satisfies only one part of readiness.

**Proposed success thresholds:** At least 60% attempted/eligible tasks and 50% successful/eligible tasks, with no unresolved material record discrepancy and no deterioration in rework or active task time against a defined baseline. These are planning proposals, not agreed targets or achieved results. Capture denominators separately by group without identifying individuals.

**Stop/fallback:** Pause affected tasks for access-control concerns, material incorrect outputs, missing support, or unreconciled spreadsheet differences. Users return to the required spreadsheet workflow under existing authority. Record affected tasks and reconcile tool/spreadsheet records before resuming; business fallback does not require assuming a software rollback. Owner and escalation contacts remain to be named.

**Old-workflow retirement:** Not authorized or planned during this pilot. Any later retirement needs process-owner approval, confirmed control-owner authority, correct downstream records, stable quality, acceptable task burden, and a workable recovery route. Wider rollout is also unapproved.
